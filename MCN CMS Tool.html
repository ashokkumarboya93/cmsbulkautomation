    How MCN CMS Tool works

    1. Select your images

    Choose files, pick a whole folder, or drag and drop. JPG, PNG, GIF and WEBP are accepted; other files are skipped with a notice.

    2. Review the list

    Check thumbnails, sizes and dimensions. Duplicates are flagged, unreadable images are left out, and you can remove any image.

    3. Metadata is generated

    Each image gets a numbered folder (001_Title) with a content.json holding the title and MIME type, and the original image in _media.

    4. ZIP is built and downloaded

    Packaging happens locally in your browser, with original image bytes untouched. Nothing is sent to any server.

    5. Import into Salesforce

    Use the ZIP in your Salesforce CMS import workflow for Marketing Cloud Next. This tool does not log in to or upload to Salesforce.
